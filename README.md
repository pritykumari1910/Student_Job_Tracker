Student Job Tracker
Overview
The Student Job Tracker is a web application designed to help students manage and track their job applications, interviews, and career-related activities. It allows students to log and monitor their progress for multiple job opportunities, track the status of applications, and organize relevant documents or notes.

Features
Job Application Management: Track job opportunities, application deadlines, and status (applied, interviewed, hired, etc.).

Interview Schedule: Keep track of upcoming interview dates and times.

Job Details: Add and store important details for each job listing (company name, role, salary, benefits, etc.).

Document Storage: Attach relevant documents such as resumes, cover letters, and job offer letters.

Notification System: Get reminders for upcoming deadlines, interviews, or follow-ups.

Search and Filter: Easily search and filter job listings by company, role, application status, etc.

User Authentication: Secure login system to keep job information private.
